The XRP Bathtub

  • The water level is the price on the tub's own scale: the bottom of the tub is $0.10, the rim is $10. It's a log scale, so each step up the tub is a multiple ($0.10 → $1 → $10 is the same climb twice). Change the scale in ⚙.
  • The faucet runs when buyers are winning: over the last minute, more dollars of XRP were bought from sellers than sold to buyers, counting every trade on Coinbase as it happens. The harder the buying, the harder it pours.
  • The drain opens when sellers are winning the minute. The harder the selling, the faster it swirls.
  • The flow also nudges the water a little above or below the price, so you can watch buying pour in and selling drain out (⚙ → Flow nudge).
  • Lines in the tub: gold is the all-time high (from CoinGecko). Add your own with ＋ Target; tap a line's label to remove it.
  • The lettering on the wall above the tub has it all: the price, the day's change, the 24-hour high, low and volume, market cap, Coinbase's volume, the all-time high (in gold) and the last minute's flow with the last ten minutes beside it. Tap it to look at it straight on; tap again to come back.

"Liquidity", "volume", "flow"

Volume is how much traded in 24 hours. Flow here is buying minus selling: on every trade one side crossed over to take the other's price, and that's who we count. Liquidity, strictly, is how much can be bought or sold without moving the price much (how deep the order book is). This first pass shows flow and volume, the closest live numbers that are free; order-book depth could come next.

Where the numbers come from

Coinbase Exchange (XRP-USD: every trade and the price, live). CoinGecko (all-time high, market cap, 24 h volume across all exchanges, every 90 s). If a feed is down you'll see — and an "offline" badge, never made-up numbers. Add ?demo to the address for a pretend market that fills and drains on its own.
