Things to try

  • Rapid fire (the Competition Pro): flip its rapid-fire switch, hold a fire button, and watch its tile: a red ⚡ shows how many presses a second the stick is sending by itself.
  • The four fire buttons: press each of the two round ones and the two red triangles in turn, and note which numbers they are. A buyer says left and right send different numbers.
  • The GameSir X5 Lite: hold M + A for 2 seconds to swap its A/B and X/Y, then press them again here and see what moved.
  • “standard” vs “raw”: standard means the browser knows the layout (A is 0, the d-pad is 12–15). Raw means it's just numbers, and that's when games guess wrong.
  • Names and picks are saved on this device for each controller. Copy the map to send it to me for the games.
