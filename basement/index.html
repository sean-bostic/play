Click an object (or its tag, or its line in the list) · Space + arrows slide it · Ctrl (or Option) + arrows turn it · Shift finer · R quarter turn · D delete (D again, from the list, brings it back) · click the empty floor to let go. Edits are kept in this browser; 💾 saves them to a file (📋 copies them). Drag with the mouse works too.
