Joysticks

    
Joystick: throttle = gas · trigger = hose, horn, loop or the train's crane (never a brake) · stick back = brake, hold to back up (the train has no brake) · other buttons honk · hold button 4 for the vehicle menu. Plane: stick back climbs. Train: stick left/right picks the track. Keys: ↑ gas · ↓ brake (not the train) · space honks · V or Tab menu · 1–5 pick · Esc or ⌘Q quits
