Joysticks

    
Joystick: stick forward walks (and the throttle) · left/right turns · back stops and backs up · trigger = whistle, woof or meow (next to someone glowing: be them) · hold any other button 1.5 s: a ring shows on the next one (keep holding to move it on: Daddy, Mia, Copper, the hawk), let go to be them. Hawk: stick back climbs, forward dives, trigger lands. Daddy: the trigger whistles (Mia and Copper follow); walk up to the truck and the trigger gets in; in it, the trigger honks; hold the left button (any but the trigger) to get everyone out. The mower: the same (it cuts the grass; sticks hop in the trailer, the woodpile tips them out). The bow: walk up to its stand, trigger picks it up; stick aims, hold the trigger to draw, let go to shoot; hold the left button to put it down. Keys: arrows · Space · Tab or C next · 1–4 pick · Esc or ⌘Q quits
